WORD / NOUN
serendipity
/ˌser.ənˈdɪp.ə.ti/　US　C1 · Rare
The pleasant discovery of something valuable or interesting by chance.
意外发现美好事物的幸运；机缘巧合带来的惊喜。
分义详解 1
01 noun

The occurrence of a happy or useful discovery when you were not looking for it.

We met by pure serendipity in a tiny bookshop in Lisbon.
我们在里斯本的一家小书店里偶然相遇，纯属美妙的巧合。
COMMON PAIRINGS
